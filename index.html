Welcome

Welcome to AK Exim and Trade Concern

We are a fully compliant, Kathmandu-based trading house specialising in high-volume imports and B2B distribution. By sourcing directly from India and diverse international markets, we supply Nepal’s retail networks with premium consumer goods while providing the financial guidance necessary to optimise trade capital and drive sustainable growth.

About us

A gateway for global goods entering Nepal

Our mission

To provide a seamless, legally compliant gateway for global goods entering the Nepalese market, while empowering our local B2B partners with strategic financial insights and reliable supply chains.

Who we are

Officially registered with the Inland Revenue Department of Nepal (PAN: 115802098) and led by Proprietor Ashish Khatri, AK Exim and Trade Concern operates at the intersection of international logistics and local commerce. Headquartered in Kirtipur Municipality, Kathmandu, we eliminate redundant intermediaries to bring cost-effective, high-demand products directly to domestic distributors and retailers.

Our services

From procurement to distribution, and the financial advice in between

Global Procurement & Imports

We manage the complexities of cross-border trade, customs compliance and international freight. Our active sourcing networks span major manufacturing hubs in India and strategic third-country markets, ensuring our supply chain remains resilient and our pricing remains highly competitive.

Wholesale Distribution

We operate a dedicated domestic distribution infrastructure. As a licensed wholesale provider, our core commercial portfolio includes:

  • Beauty & Personal CarePremium cosmetics, fragrances, soaps and skincare products.
  • Consumer ElectronicsHigh-velocity tech and home appliances.
  • FMCG & Household CommoditiesEssential daily goods and generalised commercial trade.

Financial & Trade Advisory

We go beyond traditional product supply. We leverage our deep understanding of market dynamics and import logistics to provide tailored financial guidance to our partners. We consult on:

  • Procurement budget optimisation
  • Trade finance and capital allocation
  • Inventory return on investment (ROI) maximisation
